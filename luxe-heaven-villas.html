ABOUT THE PROPERTY

Presenting two ultra-luxurious, fully furnished European-style villas in the prime location of Pehn de Franc, Porvorim.

Designed for those who seek uncompromising luxury, Luxe Heaven Villas sit majestically on a 585 Sqm plot. Every inch of this property has been carefully curated to deliver a private resort experience, combining the timeless elegance of European architecture with the vibrant soul of North Of Goa.

From the private Otis elevator and state-of-the-art smart home security to a dedicated luxury home theatre and stunning bar setups, this is a home built for exceptional living and grand entertaining.

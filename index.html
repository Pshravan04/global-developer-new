NORTH OF GOA

Why North of Goa Is Getting Stronger Attention

Goa is evolving from a seasonal holiday spot to a premier year-round destination, driven by wellness, workations, and experience-led tourism.

The region's rich heritage, thriving gastronomy, and growing MICE (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, and Exhibitions) sector are reshaping its lifestyle appeal.

The newly inaugurated Mopa Airport (Manohar International Airport) has vastly improved global and domestic connectivity, making North Goa more accessible than ever before.

Significant infrastructure upgrades, including the expansion of NH-66, are reducing travel times and transforming the region into an organized real estate hub for premium developments.
